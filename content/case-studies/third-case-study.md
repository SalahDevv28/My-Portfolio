# Case Study 3: British Engineering Team

## The Challenge

A UK-based engineering team was growing fast — but their tools weren't keeping up. Project progress was hard to track, collaboration happened across too many disconnected platforms, and managing outreach and follow-ups with clients was eating into time better spent on engineering work.

They needed a single, structured system that could bring the team together, keep projects on track, and take repetitive communication tasks off their plate.

## Our Solution

We built a fully customized CRM system designed around the team's specific workflows — combining a clean collaboration workspace with AI-powered automation for their outreach pipeline.

### 1. Centralized Collaboration Workspace

The foundation of the system is a shared workspace where the entire team operates from one place. No more switching between tools or chasing updates across email threads.

Every team member has visibility into what's happening, who owns what, and where each project stands — at any given moment.

### 2. Project & Task Tracking

We built a structured tracking system that gives the team full control over their workload:

- **Projects** — Track progress across active engineering engagements, with clear stages, owners, and timelines
- **Tasks** — Break projects down into actionable items, assigned to individuals with deadlines and priority levels
- **Alignment** — Shared views ensure the whole team stays organized and no work falls through the cracks

### 3. AI-Powered Outreach & Follow-Ups

On top of the CRM, we integrated an AI layer that handles two key areas of client communication:

**Outreach Management** — The system intelligently manages when and how the team reaches out to prospects and clients, ensuring consistent, timely contact without manual coordination.

**Follow-Up Automation** — After key interactions or project milestones, the AI triggers follow-up sequences automatically — keeping relationships warm and clients informed without adding to the team's to-do list.

## Outcome

The British engineering team now works from a single, well-structured system that keeps them aligned on every project and removes the overhead of manual client communication. Their collaboration is sharper, their projects are easier to track, and their outreach runs in the background — so the team can focus on what they do best.
